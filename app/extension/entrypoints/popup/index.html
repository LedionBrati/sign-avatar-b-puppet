A signer rides along in the corner of any tab, with captions underneath. Pick what it listens to:

  • CaptionsYouTube's transcript or CC, or any video with captions. No permission.
  • Tab audioWhat the tab plays, turned into text by your SIGNLAYER server. Starts when you pick it here.
  • MicrophoneSpeech around you, recognised by Chrome. Asks for the mic once.
  • Selected textRight-click › Sign with SIGNLAYER, or Alt+Shift+S.

Signs we don't have yet stay as captions, never guessed. Alt+Shift+K shows or hides the signer.
